Decks left

Honest odds: no system can always beat the dealer. The best possible play, which this uses, cuts the house edge to about 0.5%, so you still lose many hands and sessions. Rainbet’s Blackjack pays 3:2 on blackjack and 2:1 on insurance (confirmed on its rules page), which this matches. Rainbet doesn’t publish the dealer’s soft 17 rule or split limits, so this assumes the dealer stands on soft 17 and doubling after split is allowed. Only bet what you can afford to lose. Auto-read: on a phone, point the camera at the game on another screen (phones can’t share another app’s screen into a web page). Fill the preview with the game, hold steady, avoid glare.
